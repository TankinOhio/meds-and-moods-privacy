Privacy Policy for Meds and Moods for Bipolar

Last updated November 24, 2026

Meds and Moods for Bipolar is an Android app for recording and reviewing information about your mood, symptoms, and medications. The app stores your records on your device. You do not need to create an account.

Information you enter

You can record sleep, mood and symptom ratings, energy, appetite, psychotic symptoms, medication names and doses, medication schedules, missed doses, as-needed medication use, and notes. The app also stores check-in dates and whether a day is locked. It uses this information to display your history and create graphs and written reports on your device.

Storage and access

Check-ins and medication records are stored in an app-private database on your Android device. The app does not transmit these records to the developer or a cloud service. It does not include ads or analytics trackers, and Android's automatic app backup is disabled. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app may be able to view your records.

Sharing information you choose

You can use Android's share menu to share a written report and selected graph values, your current medication list, or both. The medication list includes names, doses, and schedules. Shared reports exclude daily notes and medication names. You choose which app receives the information and who to send it to, such as a healthcare provider. The receiving app or service may transmit, store, or handle the information under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive a copy through the app.

Local backups and phone transfers

If you choose to create a local backup, the app writes a file to a location you select on your device. The file contains your saved check-ins, notes, and medication list. It is readable without a password. Keep it in a private location, and use a local transfer method such as USB if you want the backup to stay off cloud services. You can restore the file on another phone. Restoring replaces the app records on the receiving phone after you confirm.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps records on your device until you clear its storage in Android Settings, uninstall the app, or replace its records by restoring a backup. The developer has no server copy to delete. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete backup files you saved separately or copies you shared. Delete separate backup files using your file manager. For information you shared, contact the recipient or manage the copy in the app you used to send it.

Optional donations

The Donate button opens Buy Me a Coffee in your browser. The app does not send your health records to that site. Donations and any personal or payment information you provide there are handled by Buy Me a Coffee and its payment providers.
